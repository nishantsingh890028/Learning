Privacy Policy – Khel Khel Mein

Last updated: [DATE]

Khel Khel Mein ("the app") is a learning app for children aged 3 to 10, published by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] ("we"). We take children's privacy seriously and designed the app to collect no personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no chat, no advertising and no analytics. Everything the app keeps (each child's first name or nickname, age and avatar, stars, stickers, daily progress, drawings and settings) is saved only on the device. It is never uploaded and is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when a parent uses "Reset progress".

Purchases

The optional one-time "Full Pack" purchase is processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases; it receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase record, never your child's name, contact details or location. Purchases can only be started from the parents area, which is protected by a question for adults.

Voice

Lessons, poems and stories are spoken using voice recordings stored inside the app, or your device's built-in text-to-speech. When a poem asks the child to repeat it, the child says it out loud and taps a button. No audio is recorded and the app has no microphone permission.

Third-party links

The only link in the app is to this privacy policy, inside the parents area.

Children's privacy

The app is designed to comply with Google Play's Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Because we collect no personal data from children, no parental consent is required for data collection.
