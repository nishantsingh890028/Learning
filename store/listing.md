# Play Store listing – Khel Khel Mein

**App name (30 chars max):** Khel Khel Mein: Kids Learning

**Short description (80 chars max):**
CBSE, ICSE & Cambridge-style learning games for ages 3–10: English, Hindi, Maths

**Full description:**
Khel Khel Mein (खेल खेल में, "learning through play") follows the CBSE / NCERT syllabus from Balvatika to Class 3, chapter by chapter, in English, Hindi or both. Golu the friendly elephant teaches every lesson with games, so children understand instead of memorising.

📚 THE CBSE SYLLABUS, AS GAMES – 181 chapters
• English (Mridang, Santoor): letters, phonics, vocabulary, grammar, sentences and stories
• Hindi (Sarangi, Veena): वर्णमाला, मात्राएँ, शब्द, वचन, लिंग, विलोम, कहानियाँ
• Maths (Joyful Mathematics, Maths Mela): counting to 3-digit numbers, add, subtract, tables, fractions, time, money, weight, data
• My World / EVS (Our Wondrous World): body, plants, animals, food, water, festivals, waste and safety

🧮 MATHS GYM & ABACUS – Golu solves an example step by step, then fresh practice every time: + − × ÷, tables 1–20, reverse counting, and a full abacus course
🧩 PUZZLES – mazes, sudoku, number pyramids, sliding puzzles
💛 GOOD VALUES – manners, kindness to younger ones, pets and elders, honesty, body safety and how to tell parents when something feels wrong

🏫 CBSE, ICSE AND INTERNATIONAL (CAMBRIDGE-STYLE) SYLLABUS – pick your child’s board and class
📖 28 STORIES – Panchatantra, Tenali Raman, Akbar–Birbal, Shravan Kumar and fables, each with a moral
🌍 SAVE MOTHER EARTH and 🧺 OUT & ABOUT – etiquette for picnics, travel, markets, hospitals and more

🎮 863 HANDS-ON ACTIVITIES
✍️ Draw shapes and letters – Golu shows how, then your child traces
🎨 Colouring pages, dot-to-dot pictures and a magic rangoli maker
🧺 Drag-and-drop sorting, matching and sequencing games
🔟 Ten-frames, place-value bundles, a real clock, balance scale, fair-sharing plates, a shop with coins
🎈 Balloon pop, picture hunts, stories with morals, poems Golu sings, then "your turn!"
🙏 Sanskar: greetings, respect for elders, Indian festivals · 🧭 Smart Choices: feelings, friendship and safety

🌅 A DAILY JOURNEY – 5 short activities a day (about 15 minutes) that move forward through the syllabus.

FOR PARENTS
✔ A short guide explains how the app works
✔ Daily report with "talk about it tonight" questions, plus syllabus progress for every subject
✔ Several children on one phone, each in their own class
✔ No ads. No accounts. No microphone. Works offline. Nothing leaves the phone.
✔ Daily play-time limit and a parent-only area
✔ Free to try (first chapters of every subject) – unlock everything once for ₹299, no subscription
