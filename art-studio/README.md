# Art Studio: your own in-house AI painting studio

Paint anything (Varanasi ghats, Ganga aarti, Buddha, Kerala backwaters...) in styles like palette-knife oil,
classical oil, watercolour, Madhubani, Tanjore, Pichwai, Mughal miniature and Kerala mural; customise it
("make it night", "add more boats"), turn your own photo into a painting, preview it on a wall and save it for printing.

Everything runs on your Mac. No online service, no API key, no cost per painting.

## One-time setup (about 15 minutes, mostly the model download)
1. Install **Draw Things** (free) from the Mac App Store.
2. Open it, pick the model **FLUX.1 [schnell]** and let it download. (Apache 2.0 licence: you may sell prints.)
3. Draw Things → Settings → **API Server**: switch on, HTTP, port 7860. Keep Draw Things open.
4. Double-click **Start Art Studio.command** in this folder (first time: right-click → Open).
5. The window shows two addresses. Open the first on the Mac, or the second on your phone (same Wi-Fi).

Paintings are saved in **Pictures → Art Studio**.

## Speed
A painting takes roughly 1–3 minutes on a MacBook Air, faster on M-series Pro/Max machines.
Use **Quick** while exploring ideas and **Best** for the final picture.
